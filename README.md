# Burischar Groundwater Survey

## Preliminary observations from a community survey

This repository contains preliminary results from a voluntary community survey
on the visible characteristics and household experiences associated with
tube-well water in Burischar and surrounding areas, Bangladesh.

📍 **Study area:** Burischar and surrounding areas  
📊 **Current respondents:** 15 households  
🔄 **Survey status:** Ongoing  

## Important note

These results are based on self-reported observations from a small number of
households and do not represent a laboratory-based water quality assessment.

Visible characteristics such as reddish/brown particles, staining, turbidity,
or changes after standing may be consistent with the presence or oxidation of
iron, but they do not confirm iron concentration or the presence or absence of
other contaminants.

The survey results cannot be used to determine whether water is safe for
drinking. Parameters such as iron, manganese, arsenic, nitrate, and
microbiological contamination require appropriate water testing.
